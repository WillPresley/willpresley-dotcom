As can be clearly seen by the lack of updates on this site, the last 6 months have been rather hectic for me both personally and professionally. Here's a (very) brief overview of how things have been going!

## Spring

### March

In March there was a tremendous snow storm here in Athens, and a couple days later we got <a href="https://www.facebook.com/electronic.vision.ev/posts/925654360812603" target="_blank">these pictures</a> of the town from the company quadcopter:

### April

In April I got the chance to see the Columbus Alternative Fashion Week (AFW) Grand Finale show at the Idea Foundry. The show was fantastic and the designer we were supporting did a great job.

### May

In May we moved into the bigger and nicer apartment next door to where we had lived for 3 years. It gets more sun, has much more space, and will be a perfect platform to move forward!

At the end of the month we finally _properly_ attended the <a href="http://www.nelsonvillefest.org/" target="_blank">Nelsonville Music Festival</a>, headlined in 2015 by The Flaming Lips, St. Vincent, Trampled by Turtles, and Merle Haggard. The festival is an absolute blast, and I highly recommend it.

<img src="/uploads/2015-09/nmf-flaming-lips.jpg" alt="The Flaming Lips at NMF 2015" title="The Flaming Lips at NMF 2015" />
