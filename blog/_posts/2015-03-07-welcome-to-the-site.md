Welcome to the new and improved WillPresley.com! I'm going to use this post to talk about some of the motivations behind the site itself, as well as some technical details on how I build and deploy the site.

## About Me

I think that my name is plastered on the site enough, but I'm Will. I'm currently 25 years old and live in Athens, Ohio. I have long been obsessed with computers and technology, and have been making websites since I was 10 years old.

### Websites from Long Ago

My first website that was seen by the public was a fan site and download center for the PC game series [RollerCoaster Tycoon](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RollerCoaster_Tycoon){:target="_blank"}. Called **RCT Market** and launched originally on GeoCities sometime around 2001, it first appears in the Archive.org Wayback Machine in April of 2002 in a v2 custom PHP incarnation: [link here](http://web.archive.org/web/20020426014315/http://rctmarket.rctoa.com/){:target="_blank"}. The whole experience of building and managing both a site and a community was a fantastic opportunity for a 12-13 year old, and set the foundations of my future career choice.

After RCT Market, I focused mostly on school and playing soccer for quite a few years. One semi-successful website I managed to launch during this time was a general purpose gaming hub called GamingChamber.com. Gaming Chamber was, at its peak, a community of around 200 users who discussed all aspects of console and PC gaming, and also regularly joined up to play with or against each other in online games. One of the final initiatives of the site staff before before the site faded out of existence was a short-lived, two-issue "magazine" for the Sony PSP called I^2, one of the first (and in retrospect, last) of its kind.

During my later high school years, I reached out a couple of guys from England who were already working on the PC Gaming-focused website De-Frag.com. They seemed like they needed a good site administrator and developer, so I offered them my services and we worked together for close to two years. I also contributed to the writing team for both news and reviews, a similar 'Programmer/Writer' role to those I performed on both RCT Market and Gaming Chamber.

### What I Do Now

As of 2015, I am a professional web developer for <a href="http://www.ev.net/" target="_blank">Electronic Vision</a>, a multimedia company in Athens with a long and rich history of being the most cutting-edge shop in the area. A large portion of my projects for EV are full web rebuilds for clients in one of a number of platforms including Wordpress, CommonSpot, Moodle, MantisBT, Drupal, and more.
