In what is clearly becoming a recurring theme for this site and blog, I've been extremely busy both with work and with life, so the updates will have to come as a large chunk yet again!

## Fall

### September

In September I finally got back out onto the soccer field after many years away, captaining a team named Sherwood Forest in Athens Community Soccer. We also started regularly going to <a href="https://www.facebook.com/littlefishbrewing" target="_blank">Little Fish Brewing</a> which is over past White's Mill on the far west side of Athens.

<img src="/uploads/2016-06/littlefish-release.jpg" alt="Little Fish Brewing new beer release." title="Little Fish Brewing new beer release." />

In late September we had the fabulous pleasure of seeing <a href="http://www.thewoodbros.com/" target="_blank">The Wood Brothers</a> at Nelsonville's very own Stuart's Opera House. It was my very first time stepping foot in the venue, and I've already been back a few times since!

<img src="/uploads/2016-06/woodbros-stuarts.jpg" alt="The Wood Brothers at Stuart's Opera House on September 28th, 2015" title="The Wood Brothers at Stuart's Opera House on September 28th, 2015" />

### October

October in Athens is summed up by two very, very big events: Homecoming and Halloween. Halloween may be bigger, and more well known, but it is Homecoming that is truly the best day in Athens of the entire year.

<a class="image-link" href="https://www.ohio.edu/compass/stories/15-16/10/Homecoming-2015-Highlights.cfm" target="_blank"><img src="/uploads/2016-05/homecoming-2015.jpg" alt="Homecoming Parade, 2015" title="Homecoming Parade, 2015" /></a>

In mid-October, the starts *finally* aligned, and we were able to see Modest Mouse. It was in Columbus outdoors at the LC (now EXPRESS LIVE!) and was a magical, amazing night.

<img src="/uploads/2016-06/mm-columbus.jpg" alt="Modest Mouse at the LC on October 16th, 2015" title="Modest Mouse at the LC on October 16th, 2015" />
