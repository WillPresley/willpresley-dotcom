## Spring

### March

Springtime in Athens is always fantastic, and this one was no exception. After some much-needed fort building and hermit-holing at the apartment, the creative juices started flowing again.

<a class="image-link" href="https://www.instagram.com/p/BDedYnuke2Q/" target="_blank"><img src="/uploads/2016-05/westsidebestside-wall.jpg" alt="The wall outside of our apartment facing the road." title="The wall outside of our apartment facing the road." /></a>

The run of amazing concerts was further extended by catching Dr. Dog at the Newport. The show was honestly incredible, and they are worth seeing over and over.

<img src="/uploads/2016-05/drdog-columbus.jpg" alt="Dr. Dog playing at the Newport on March 29th, 2016." title="Dr. Dog playing at the Newport on March 29th, 2016." />

### April

In April work _finally_ started on the garden/flowerbed outside of the new apartment. I will have actual pictures of things growing soon, but needless to say, it's been a success.

<a class="image-link" href="https://ohioishome.com" target="_blank"><img src="/uploads/2016-05/garden-beer.jpg" alt="One of my favorite beers along with an opener from ohioishome.com" title="One of my favorite beers along with an opener from ohioishome.com" /></a>

We also returned to Louisville, Kentucky for Thunder Over Louisville, where lots of carnival-type things were done and I took pictures for an upcoming project.

<img src="/uploads/2016-05/thunder-fireworks.jpg" alt="Fireworks over the midway in Louisville." title="Fireworks over the midway in Louisville." />

And lastly for April was one of the better Sunday Funday events at the <a href="http://jackieos.com/" target="_blank">Jackie O's</a> Taproom, a beach party featuring some of our favorite local bands like Water Witches and The D-Rays!

<img src="/uploads/2016-05/sunday-funday.jpg" alt="Sunday Funday at Jackie O's featuring The D-Rays." title="Sunday Funday at Jackie O's featuring The D-Rays." />

### May

May has been, thankfully, quite a bit more relaxed than a lot of months. This is just the calm before the storm, however, as Nelsonville Music Festival and Bonnaroo are both _just_ around the corner! One thing that **has** come to Athens in huge numbers, for the first time in 17 years, is the cicada. There are thousands and thousands of <a href="http://www.magicicada.org/databases/magicicada/map.html" target="_blank">confirmed reports</a> in our area, and there are at least a few on our property already as well.

<img src="/uploads/2016-05/bush-cicada.jpg" alt="A cicada hanging out in the bush by our front porch." title="A cicada hanging out in the bush by our front porch." />
